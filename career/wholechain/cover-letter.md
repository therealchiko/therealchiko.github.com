# Chiko Mukwenha

Senior Frontend React Engineer | Wholechain

Dear Wholechain team,

I’m applying because your role gives engineering a part in shaping how the product works and feels. That is how I like to work. I love simple, effective functionality, and I care about the decisions underneath it that make it dependable. A question I often ask is: *What would it look like if Apple built this?* For me, that means working through the complexity until the experience makes sense to the person using it.

At JaSure, where I was one of two mobile engineering leads, we took a React Native insurance app from prototype to launch. When COVID hit and cars stopped moving, demand for pay-as-you-go cover increased sharply. I moved quickly to integrate in-app support, real-time vehicle valuations, and switching cover on and off across systems. I also led a UI overhaul informed by session recordings and conversations with the product team. The work connected what people needed on a screen to what had to happen underneath it.

At Customer.io, I was part of the Platforms team and made substantial contributions to migrating a large application from Ember to React, including customisations to the React-Ember bridge. I built and maintained the design component library, worked with atomic CSS, Storybook, and Jest, and contributed extensively to frontend performance improvements. FullStory and Mixpanel helped inform product and UX decisions. I also designed and shipped a multilingual subscription centre. Accessible forms and keyboard navigation were priorities, and paying attention to WCAG guidance is something I take pride in.

I bring deep backend experience to frontend work. I want to understand the whole path from data and business logic to the interface, including what happens when something fails. At Customer.io, that included high-throughput messaging infrastructure and asynchronous processing. For ContentBoost, the Shopify app I built and later sold, I chose Go for its efficient concurrency model, running catalogue ingestion and LLM generation jobs concurrently. I owned the Next.js interface, Go APIs, SQL migrations, Shopify integration, and billing. Understanding those layers helps me make better decisions about the experience they support.

Today I’m building Hydra, a retention platform for South African merchants, using React through Next.js, Go, and Flutter. It brings together WhatsApp, rewards, campaigns, and merchant tools. I also use specialist AI coding agents, giving them shared product context and checking their work through independent review and testing. Working this way has reinforced how much the quality of the outcome depends on understanding the problem and carefully verifying the implementation.

I’m self-taught, curious, and particular about my work. I enjoy taking an uncertain problem through to a finished product, working with others to understand the “why”, and following that through to the details. I’d welcome a conversation about Wholechain’s product and how I could contribute to its frontend, design system, and the engineering behind it.

Sincerely,
Chiko Mukwenha
