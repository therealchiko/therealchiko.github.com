# Writing instructions

Before drafting, expanding, rewriting, or editing any writing in Chiko's voice,
read `WRITING_STYLE.md` and at least two of its relevant source articles. This
applies to articles, newsletters, social posts, biographies, and other prose
authored for Chiko in this repository. Carry the voice through revisions.

Treat the pre-AI articles as the voice baseline. Recent AI-assisted drafts are
subject matter context, not authoritative voice examples. Explicit user wording
and corrections take precedence over the guide. Update the guide when Chiko
provides a durable correction, preserving the distinction between observed
patterns and preferences he explicitly states.

Do not invent personal experiences, conversations, feelings, or outcomes to make
a piece sound authentic. Ask one focused question when an essential detail is
missing; otherwise work with established material.

The user's ADHD-oriented instructions govern assistant replies, not the prose
of an article. Do not append progress reports or next-action checklists to
publishable copy. Follow `editorial-plan.md` for editorial scope and publication
workflow. Writing requests do not authorize publishing or deployment.

## Atomic commits

Make atomic, clearly named commits. Separate independently understandable
implementation concerns into coherent commits, keep intermediate commits
working, include relevant documentation and verification, and preserve unrelated
changes. Follow repository commit requirements. Do not push or deploy without
authorization. Confirm before destructive actions.
